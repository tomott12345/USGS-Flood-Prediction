Train a new site

Runs xgboost_model/auto_pipeline.py: discovers upstream gages via USGS's NLDI network graph, auto-selects baseline vs. upstream+weather-enriched+tuned features per horizon by comparing held-out accuracy, and saves the winner as a deployable model. A cold run (no cached upstream-gage scan yet) can take several minutes.

8-15 digits. Find yours at waterdata.usgs.gov.

Forecast horizons
Longer horizons (not recommended)

This repo's own backtesting found NSE goes negative — worse than just predicting the mean — at 12h and beyond, for every feature set and tuning configuration tried so far. See xgboost_model/README.md. Enable these to re-test that on a new site, not because they're expected to perform well.

USGS's instantaneous-values service generally retains ~120 days of sub-daily history.
